Method

How it works

ProbaLab runs a proprietary data model for each sport. For the NBA it rates every player from recent box scores, weighted toward recent games. Before each game it reads the official injury report, works out the minutes each team is likely to play, and turns that into a team strength for tonight. That is blended with a long-run team rating, recent form, rest and back-to-backs to give each side a win probability.

The model was tested on the 2025–26 season game by game, using only information available before each tip-off. Its percentages lined up with real results: picks given 70–75% won 74% of the time.

The lights

Super green

The model is confident (60%+) and rates the team well above the market. Our strongest tips.

Green

The model gives the pick 65% or more. Also a tip.

Orange

A lean, 55–65%. Shown for completeness, not a tip.

Red

Under 55%. Close to a coin flip.

Fair and value prices

Fair price is what the win probability is worth as decimal odds: 1 ÷ probability. A 70% pick has a fair price of $1.43. Value at $X or better adds a 3% margin. A price below it isn't worth taking on our numbers.

Beating the close

For tips, we compare the price at the time of the tip with the final market price at tip-off. Beating the close consistently is the clearest sign a prediction is ahead of the market. We show it as a percentage after each game.
